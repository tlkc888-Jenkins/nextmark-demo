Why dumb OCR fails

      
Edge cases found in these very SIs

      
    
        
  • The Clearing Mark has no coordinates.Laid ~0.8 nm upwind on the day; rounding side signalled by a red or green flag on the start boat — no flag means it's deleted. The app needs a two-tap on-the-day input (the flag control above), or a photo of the start boat's flag.
    • 
        
  • Courses 1–6 use relative marks.W/L marks (1, 1a, Gate 3p/3s) are laid to windward with only a bearing board on the RC vessel — and Division 1 and 2 round different marks on the same course number. The app must know your division.
    • 
        
  • Course 24 is "X1 — as described at briefing."No machine-readable definition exists anywhere. Needs a manual course builder as fallback.
    • 
        
  • A baked-in rules change (RRS 34).If a mark is missing or >0.1 nm off station, boats round its published coordinates. A GPS app can actually guide this — paper can't.
    • 
        
  • Gates, not points."Gate 3p/3s — either mark may be rounded." Rounding logic must accept a pair.
    • 
        
  • No signal past the breakers.GPS is satellite-based and needs no mobile network — only the AI extraction does. So: ingest ashore (or near the start line), cache course, marks and charts on the device, and queue any photo taken offline for extraction when signal returns. Try the signal toggle above — racing never stops.
    • 
        
  • Approximate everything.Start and finish share one "approx." coordinate; the Course Book labels its own diagrams "not for navigation." Verification screen before race day is non-negotiable.
