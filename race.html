Course

Sunday offshore passage racing is course X1 — a course set by the Race Committee and given at the Compulsory Skippers Briefing. Build it here from the board, in the order you sail it.

You are in crew mode, so this course is read-only — it comes from the helm's shared link. Turn crew mode off in Settings if you need to build your own.

Settings

Crew mode

On a boat only one person runs the start. Crew mode follows the course the helm shared, and hides the start sequence, the line pin and course editing so eight phones cannot drift apart. Your own track, numbers and roundings are unaffected.

Your boat

Used on the race card and in the SI 20.8 rounding declaration.

Wind direction

Optional, and only used to place the laid clearance buoy upwind of the start line. Your instruments are the real source — changing this never touches your track.

degrees the wind is from

Reading the board automatically

Optional. The key is stored on this device only and is sent straight to the API — never to us. Leave it empty and build the course by hand; everything else works the same.

What this app is not

Not for navigation. Mark positions are the published SI coordinates and the coastline is a generalised outline — use your chartplotter and your eyes. GPS recording stops whenever the screen locks or the app is backgrounded, which is why the app holds the screen awake while racing; any gap in recording is drawn as a gap, never bridged with a straight line.
